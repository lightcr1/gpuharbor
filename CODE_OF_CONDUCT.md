# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want this project to be a place where anyone can ask a question without being
made to feel stupid. Contributors and maintainers pledge to make participation
harassment-free for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity,
gender identity or expression, experience level, nationality, personal
appearance, race, religion or sexual identity.

## Expected behaviour

- Be direct about code, kind about people.
- Assume the other person is trying to help.
- Accept that "it works on my machine" is not a bug report.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Insults, personal attacks, or dismissive comments about someone's skill.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome attention of any kind.
- Publishing someone's private information without permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions.

## Scope

This applies in issues, pull requests, discussions and any other project space,
and when representing the project in public.

## Enforcement

Report problems privately to the maintainer. Reports are reviewed and answered.
Responses range from a warning to a permanent ban, depending on severity and
pattern. Maintainers who do not follow this code may be removed by other
maintainers.
